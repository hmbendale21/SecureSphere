Frequently Asked Questions

How Can We Help?

Find answers to common Contact and Support questions.

How can I contact SecureSphere?

You can use the contact form on this page to send a question, suggestion, feedback, or support request.

How can I report a security issue?

Use the Security Report section and provide a clear description of the issue, affected feature, and steps required to reproduce it.

Can I suggest a cybersecurity topic?

Yes. Use the contact form to suggest new cybersecurity learning topics, tools, features, or improvements.

Can I report a problem with a tool?

Yes. Include the tool name, what happened, what you expected to happen, and any useful steps that can help reproduce the problem.

What information should I avoid sharing?

Never include passwords, private keys, authentication tokens, financial information, or other sensitive personal information in a support message.
